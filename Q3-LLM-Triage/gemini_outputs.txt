TEST 1 – WATER LEAK
============================================================

Input message:

There is a water leak in Building C that has been running all morning.

Gemini output:

{
  "issue_category": "FACILITIES",
  "urgency": "HIGH",
  "sentiment": "NEGATIVE",
  "followup_required": "Y",
  "recommended_team": "Facilities",
  "escalation_reason": "Ongoing water leak in Building C requires prompt operational attention to reduce water waste and the risk of property damage.",
  "data_sensitivity_risk": "Low",

}

Evaluation:

The model correctly identified the water leak as a facilities issue. HIGH urgency was reasonable because the problem had continued for several hours and could cause water waste or physical damage. The Facilities team was an appropriate routing decision.

============================================================
OVERALL OBSERVATION
============================================================

Gemini produced valid structured outputs for all three messages and maintained the requested ESG categories and urgency scale. The model correctly identified the relevant organisational team in each case.

The model was also able to provide short contextual escalation reasons rather than relying only on keywords. This demonstrates an advantage over a basic rule-based classifier.

However, the results still require human oversight. Urgency and escalation decisions involve organisational judgement, and a production system should not rely on the LLM alone for high-risk cases.
