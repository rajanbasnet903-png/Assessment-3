Q4 – NOTEBOOKLM CRITICAL OBSERVATIONS

NotebookLM was evaluated for accuracy, relevance, academic usefulness, source grounding and limitations.


1. SOURCE INGESTION

Accuracy:
High

Usefulness:
The system allowed Microsoft and Unilever sustainability documents to be analysed within one notebook.

Limitation:
The quality of the analysis depends on the quality and completeness of the uploaded source material.


2. SUMMARISATION

Accuracy:
Generally high for identifying major sustainability topics.

Usefulness:
Summaries reduced the amount of time needed to identify key information across long reports.

Limitation:
Detailed targets, qualifications or contextual differences can be simplified when complex information is condensed.


3. SOURCE-GROUNDED QUESTION ANSWERING

Accuracy:
High when questions could be answered directly from the uploaded material.

Usefulness:
Focused questions made it easier to locate relevant ESG information.

Limitation:
The user still needs to check whether the cited source fully supports the AI-generated interpretation.


4. THEMATIC ANALYSIS

Accuracy:
Good

Usefulness:
NotebookLM helped identify recurring themes across Microsoft and Unilever's sustainability information.

Limitation:
Theme selection is generated by AI and may reflect the way the system interprets the documents rather than the only possible academic interpretation.


5. DOCUMENT COMPARISON

Accuracy:
Good

Usefulness:
The tool quickly highlighted similarities and differences across two large sustainability reports.

Limitation:
Some organisational context or reporting nuance may be lost when comparisons are shortened.


6. CITATION SUPPORT

Accuracy:
Useful for tracing information back to the source.

Usefulness:
Source references make the generated response more transparent and easier to verify.

Limitation:
A citation does not automatically guarantee that every interpretation made by the AI is correct. The original section should still be reviewed.


7. HALLUCINATION AND EVIDENCE RISK

NotebookLM reduces some hallucination risk by grounding responses in uploaded sources. However, AI-generated summaries and interpretations can still simplify, misinterpret or overstate information.

Unsupported predictions should not be treated as evidence.


8. BIAS

The output depends heavily on the documents provided to the system. If the source collection contains only corporate sustainability reports, the resulting analysis may reflect the organisations' own reporting perspectives.

Independent academic or regulatory sources may therefore be needed for broader critical research.


9. OVER-RELIANCE

NotebookLM can make research faster, but students should not use it as a replacement for reading key source material.

Important claims, statistics and conclusions should be verified before being included in academic work.


OVERALL FEASIBILITY

NotebookLM is suitable for controlled university use as a supporting academic research and learning tool.

It is particularly useful for organising sources, generating summaries, comparing documents and answering focused questions.

However, human judgement remains necessary.

Students should verify important outputs, consult the original documents and avoid relying entirely on AI-generated summaries for academic conclusions.
