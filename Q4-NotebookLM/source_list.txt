EXPERIMENT PURPOSE

The two documents were selected because they represent sustainability reporting from organisations operating in different industries.

Microsoft represents the technology sector, while Unilever represents the consumer goods sector.

Using both sources allowed NotebookLM to be tested for summarisation, thematic analysis, comparison and source-grounded academic question answering.
